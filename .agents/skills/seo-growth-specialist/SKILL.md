# Skill: SEO & B2B Growth Engineering Specialist

Governs organic search dominance, technical SEO, high-intent keyword capture, Local SEO (Florida restaurant & business ecosystem), and direct-response conversion funnel architecture.

## 1. Technical SEO & Indexation Protocol
- **Canonical & Domain Consistency:** Strict canonicalization to official verified domain (`https://www.exoadvancellc.com`).
- **Core Web Vitals:** Strict LCP (< 2.5s), INP (< 200ms), and CLS (< 0.1).
- **Structured Data (Schema.org JSON-LD):**
  - `SoftwareApplication`: Defines OrderPoint, Exo Server, and BI dashboards with ratings, category, and pricing.
  - `LocalBusiness` / `ProfessionalService`: Establishes physical and operational authority in Florida for local ranking.
  - `FAQPage`: Captures Google rich snippets and PAA (People Also Ask) real estate on SERPs.
- **Dynamic Sitemap & Robots:** Priority-based crawling with clean hygiene (excluding `/admin/` and private API routes).

## 2. High-Intent Keyword Strategy & Commercial Intent

  - *"kiosko de autoservicio para restaurantes Tampa / Miami"*

- **Content Clusters & Programmatic Pages:**

  - Direct comparison pages (e.g., *OrderPoint vs Toast*, *OrderPoint vs Clover*).
