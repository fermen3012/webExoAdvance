# Skill: Local SEO & Google Maps Audit (Exo Advance Lead Magnet)

Governs local search ranking optimization, Google Business Profile (GBP) audits, Maps 3-pack visibility, and automated conversion hooks for Florida restaurants.

## 1. Local Search Visibility Protocol
- **Primary & Secondary Categories:** Ensure restaurants use tight, specific categories (e.g., "Pizza restaurant", "Fast food restaurant", "Bar & grill") instead of generic categories.
- **Direct Menu Link:** Eliminate links pointing to third-party delivery services (DoorDash, UberEats, Grubhub) that extract 25-30% take-rates. Prioritize direct OrderPoint digital menu links.
- **Review Velocity & Recency:** Audit the volume and frequency of reviews. Connect checkout/kiosk flows to prompt happy guests for 5-star Google reviews.
- **Schema.org Restaurant Markup:** Validate `Restaurant` and `Menu` JSON-LD schemas so Google surfaces rich menu snippets on search results.

## 2. Commercial Outreach & Hook Angle
- **Financial Pain Framing:** Convert local ranking gaps into lost table spend (e.g., *"Losing 45 to 75 walk-in diners per month = ~$1,400 to $2,100 USD lost"*).
- **Zero-Friction Gift:** Deliver a 1-page complimentary audit report before pitching OrderPoint hardware.
- **OrderPoint Kiosk Upsell Hook:**
  > *"Nuestro Kiosko Samsung de 11 pulgadas tiene un módulo que solicita automáticamente una reseña en Google a cada comensal feliz al momento de pagar, subiendo tu puntaje en 30 días mientras ahorras en comisiones de Toast"*.
